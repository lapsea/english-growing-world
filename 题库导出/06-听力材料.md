# 听力材料

共 6 组听力节目（21 道配套测验题），录音文本与测验题均为原创。

## Greeting Time（打招呼时间）

- **所属单元：** Starter Unit 1 Hello!（你好！）
- **类型：** 句子听力 ｜ **主题：** 问候与告别 ｜ **难度：** ★ ｜ **时长：** 约 3 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| hello | 你好 |
| goodbye | 再见 |
| morning | 早晨 |
| name | 名字 |
| spell | 拼写 |
| meet | 结识；遇见 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hi! I'm Amy. | 你好！我是埃米。 |
| 2 | Hello! My name is Peter. | 你好！我叫彼得。 |
| 3 | Good morning, Miss Gao! | 早上好，高老师！ |
| 4 | How are you? | 你好吗？ |
| 5 | I'm fine, thank you. And you? | 我很好，谢谢。你呢？ |
| 6 | What's your name? | 你叫什么名字？ |
| 7 | Nice to meet you, Emma. | 很高兴认识你，埃玛。 |
| 8 | How do you spell it? | 它怎么拼写？ |
| 9 | E-M-M-A, Emma. | E-M-M-A，埃玛。 |
| 10 | Goodbye! See you tomorrow! | 再见！明天见！ |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 3 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Which sentence can you use in the morning?

- A. Good morning, Miss Gao!
- B. Good night, Miss Gao!
- C. Goodbye, Miss Gao!
- D. Nice to meet you, Miss Gao!

**答案：** A. Good morning, Miss Gao!

**解析：** 录音第 3 句 'Good morning, Miss Gao!' 是早上见面的问候语；Good night 用于晚上道别，Goodbye 用于告别，Nice to meet you 用于初次见面。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 8 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** When you don't know how to write a name, you can ask:

- A. What's your name?
- B. How are you?
- C. How do you spell it?
- D. How old are you?

**答案：** C. How do you spell it?

**解析：** 录音第 8 句 'How do you spell it?' 意为'它怎么拼写？'，不知道单词或名字怎么写时用这句话询问。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 7 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: Nice to ______ you, Emma.

**答案：** meet

**解析：** 录音第 7 句 'Nice to meet you.' 意为'很高兴认识你'，初次见面问候用动词 meet。

## New Friends at School（校园里的新朋友）

- **所属单元：** Unit 1 You and Me（你和我）
- **类型：** 情景对话 ｜ **主题：** 结交朋友 ｜ **难度：** ★★ ｜ **时长：** 约 3 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| new student | 新同学 |
| spell | 拼写 |
| class | 班级 |
| grade | 年级 |
| ping-pong | 乒乓球 |
| friend | 朋友 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 说话人 | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bill | Hi! I'm Bill. Are you a new student here? | 你好！我是比尔。你是这里的新同学吗？ |
| 2 | Meimei | Yes, I am. My name is Meimei. Nice to meet you. | 是的。我叫美美，很高兴认识你。 |
| 3 | Bill | Nice to meet you, too. How do you spell "Meimei"? | 我也很高兴认识你。"美美"怎么拼写？ |
| 4 | Meimei | M-E-I-M-E-I. It's easy. | M-E-I-M-E-I，很简单。 |
| 5 | Bill | Cool! How old are you, Meimei? | 真棒！美美，你多大了？ |
| 6 | Meimei | I'm twelve. I'm in Class 3, Grade 7. | 我十二岁，在七年级三班。 |
| 7 | Bill | Me too! Do you like sports? | 我也是！你喜欢运动吗？ |
| 8 | Meimei | Yes, I love playing ping-pong. | 喜欢，我特别爱打乒乓球。 |
| 9 | Bill | Great! I like ping-pong too. Let's play after school. | 太好了！我也喜欢乒乓球。放学后一起打吧。 |
| 10 | Meimei | OK! We can be good friends. | 好啊！我们可以成为好朋友。 |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 5 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Bill asks 'How old are you?'. What does he want to know?

- A. Meimei's age
- B. Meimei's name
- C. Meimei's class
- D. Meimei's hobby

**答案：** A. Meimei's age

**解析：** 对话第 5 轮 Bill 问 'How old are you?'，这是询问年龄的句型，所以 Bill 想知道的是美美的年龄。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 6 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** What class is Meimei in?

- A. Class 3, Grade 7
- B. Class 7, Grade 3
- C. Class 3, Grade 6
- D. Class 6, Grade 7

**答案：** A. Class 3, Grade 7

**解析：** 对话第 6 轮美美说 'I'm in Class 3, Grade 7.'，即七年级三班。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 8 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** What does Meimei love playing?

- A. Ping-pong
- B. Basketball
- C. Football
- D. Chess

**答案：** A. Ping-pong

**解析：** 对话第 8 轮美美说 'I love playing ping-pong.'，她喜欢打乒乓球。

### 测验 4（对应录音第 10 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: We can be good ______.

**答案：** friends

**解析：** 对话最后一轮美美说 'We can be good friends.'，'成为好朋友'用名词复数 friends。

## Around the New School（逛逛新学校）

- **所属单元：** Unit 3 My School（我的学校）
- **类型：** 情景对话 ｜ **主题：** 学校场所 ｜ **难度：** ★★ ｜ **时长：** 约 4 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| classroom | 教室 |
| library | 图书馆 |
| playground | 操场 |
| dining hall | 食堂 |
| gym | 体育馆 |
| next to | 在……旁边 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 说话人 | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Peter | Good morning, Ms Gao! I'm new here. Where is our classroom? | 早上好，高老师！我是新来的。我们的教室在哪里？ |
| 2 | Ms Gao | Good morning, Peter. It's on the second floor. Follow me, please. | 早上好，彼得。教室在二楼，请跟我来。 |
| 3 | Peter | Wow, the school is so big! | 哇，学校真大呀！ |
| 4 | Ms Gao | Yes. Look! This is our library. There are many interesting books in it. | 是啊。看！这是我们的图书馆，里面有许多有趣的书。 |
| 5 | Peter | Great! I love reading. Where is the playground? | 太好了！我喜欢看书。操场在哪里？ |
| 6 | Ms Gao | It's behind the library. We play sports there after class. | 操场在图书馆后面，课后我们都在那里做运动。 |
| 7 | Ms Gao | And that building is the dining hall. We have lunch there at twelve. | 那栋楼是食堂，我们十二点在那里吃午饭。 |
| 8 | Peter | Is there a gym in our school? | 学校里有体育馆吗？ |
| 9 | Ms Gao | Yes, there is. It's next to the dining hall. | 有，体育馆就在食堂旁边。 |
| 10 | Peter | Cool! I love this school! | 太棒了！我喜欢这所学校！ |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 2 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Where is the classroom?

- A. On the first floor
- B. On the second floor
- C. Next to the dining hall
- D. Behind the library

**答案：** B. On the second floor

**解析：** 对话第 2 轮老师说 'It's on the second floor.'，教室在二楼。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 4 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: This is our ______. There are many interesting books in it.

**答案：** library

**解析：** 对话第 4 轮老师介绍'这是我们的图书馆，里面有许多有趣的书'，有许多书的地方是图书馆 library。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 6 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Where is the playground?

- A. In front of the classroom
- B. Behind the library
- C. Next to the gym
- D. On the second floor

**答案：** B. Behind the library

**解析：** 对话第 6 轮老师说操场在 'behind the library'（图书馆后面），课后大家在操场做运动。

### 测验 4（对应录音第 9 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Is there a gym in the school?

- A. Yes, it is.
- B. Yes, there is.
- C. No, there isn't.
- D. We don't know.

**答案：** B. Yes, there is.

**解析：** 对话第 9 轮 Peter 问 'Is there a gym in our school?'，老师回答 'Yes, there is.'，体育馆在食堂旁边。there be 句型的一般疑问句要用 there is 回答。

## Join a Club!（一起去社团）

- **所属单元：** Unit 5 Fun Clubs（有趣的社团）
- **类型：** 情景对话 ｜ **主题：** 社团招新 ｜ **难度：** ★★ ｜ **时长：** 约 3 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| club | 社团 |
| chess | 国际象棋 |
| art club | 美术社 |
| music band | 乐队 |
| join | 加入 |
| can | 会；能 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 说话人 | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lucy | Hi, Jack! Our school has many interesting clubs. | 你好，杰克！我们学校有许多有趣的社团。 |
| 2 | Jack | Really? What clubs can we join? | 真的吗？我们可以加入哪些社团？ |
| 3 | Lucy | There's a chess club, an art club and a music band. | 有象棋社、美术社，还有一支乐队。 |
| 4 | Jack | I like chess. I can play it very well. | 我喜欢象棋，而且下得很不错。 |
| 5 | Lucy | Great! Then you can join the chess club. | 太好了！那你可以加入象棋社呀。 |
| 6 | Jack | What about you? Can you play chess? | 你呢？你会下象棋吗？ |
| 7 | Lucy | No, I can't. But I can draw very well. | 我不会，不过我画画很棒。 |
| 8 | Jack | Wonderful! Then the art club is just right for you. | 太厉害了！那美术社正适合你。 |
| 9 | Lucy | Yes! And my brother can sing and play the guitar. He wants to join the music band. | 对！我哥哥会唱歌、会弹吉他，他想加入乐队。 |
| 10 | Jack | Cool! Let's go and join our clubs together! | 太好了！我们一起去加入各自的社团吧！ |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 4 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** What can Jack do very well?

- A. Play chess
- B. Draw pictures
- C. Sing songs
- D. Play the guitar

**答案：** A. Play chess

**解析：** 对话第 4 轮 Jack 说 'I like chess. I can play it very well.'，他会下象棋，而且下得很好。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 8 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** Which club is right for Lucy?

- A. The chess club
- B. The art club
- C. The music band
- D. No club

**答案：** B. The art club

**解析：** 对话第 7-8 轮，Lucy 说自己不会下棋但画画很棒（I can draw very well），Jack 因此说美术社正适合她。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 9 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: Lucy's brother can sing and play the ______.

**答案：** guitar

**解析：** 对话第 9 轮 Lucy 说她哥哥会唱歌、弹吉他，'弹吉他'用 play the guitar，填 guitar。

## My Busy Morning（我忙碌的早晨）

- **所属单元：** Unit 6 A Day in the Life（生活中的一天）
- **类型：** 句子听力 ｜ **主题：** 日常作息 ｜ **难度：** ★ ｜ **时长：** 约 2 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| get up | 起床 |
| brush teeth | 刷牙 |
| breakfast | 早餐 |
| go to school | 去上学 |
| seven o'clock | 七点钟 |
| late | 迟到的 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I usually get up at six thirty. | 我通常六点半起床。 |
| 2 | Then I brush my teeth and wash my face. | 然后我刷牙、洗脸。 |
| 3 | I put on my school clothes quickly. | 我很快穿上校服。 |
| 4 | I have breakfast at seven o'clock. | 我七点钟吃早饭。 |
| 5 | I often have some bread and milk. | 我经常吃面包、喝牛奶。 |
| 6 | After breakfast, I read English for ten minutes. | 早饭后，我读十分钟英语。 |
| 7 | At half past seven, I go to school by bus. | 七点半，我坐公交车去上学。 |
| 8 | School starts at eight. | 学校八点开始上课。 |
| 9 | I'm never late for school. | 我上学从不迟到。 |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 1 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** What time does the speaker usually get up?

- A. At six o'clock
- B. At six thirty
- C. At seven
- D. At seven thirty

**答案：** B. At six thirty

**解析：** 录音第 1 句 'I usually get up at six thirty.'，即通常六点半起床。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 4 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: I have breakfast at ______ o'clock.

**答案：** seven

**解析：** 录音第 4 句 'I have breakfast at seven o'clock.'，七点钟吃早饭，填数字 seven。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 8 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** When does school start?

- A. At seven o'clock
- B. At seven thirty
- C. At eight
- D. At eight thirty

**答案：** C. At eight

**解析：** 录音第 8 句 'School starts at eight.'，学校八点开始上课。

## Birthday Party Shopping（生日派对采购）

- **所属单元：** Unit 7 Happy Birthday!（生日快乐！）
- **类型：** 情景对话 ｜ **主题：** 生日与购物 ｜ **难度：** ★★★ ｜ **时长：** 约 4 分钟

**听力关键词：**

| 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| birthday cake | 生日蛋糕 |
| candle | 蜡烛 |
| balloon | 气球 |
| candy | 糖果 |
| juice | 果汁 |
| how much | 多少钱 |

**录音原文：**

| # | 说话人 | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mom | Anna, your birthday is coming. Let's buy some things for your party. | 安娜，你的生日快到了，我们去给派对买些东西吧。 |
| 2 | Anna | Great, Mom! First, we need a big birthday cake. | 太好了，妈妈！首先我们需要一个大的生日蛋糕。 |
| 3 | Mom | OK. How many candles do you want? | 好。你想要多少支蜡烛？ |
| 4 | Anna | Twelve, because I'm twelve this year. | 十二支，因为我今年就十二岁了。 |
| 5 | Mom | All right. What else do we need? | 好的。我们还需要买什么？ |
| 6 | Anna | We need some balloons. They can make my room beautiful. | 我们需要一些气球，它们能把房间装扮得很漂亮。 |
| 7 | Mom | How about this bag of candy? Your friends love it. | 这袋糖果怎么样？你的朋友们都很喜欢。 |
| 8 | Anna | Good idea! And let's get some orange juice, too. | 好主意！我们再买一些橙汁吧。 |
| 9 | Mom | OK. And how much is the cake? | 好。那蛋糕多少钱呢？ |
| 10 | Anna | It's fifty yuan. It looks nice and it's not expensive. | 五十元。看起来很漂亮，也不算贵。 |

**配套测验：**

### 测验 1（对应录音第 4 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** How many candles does Anna want?

- A. Ten
- B. Eleven
- C. Twelve
- D. Thirteen

**答案：** C. Twelve

**解析：** 对话第 4 轮 Anna 说 'Twelve, because I'm twelve this year.'，她要十二支蜡烛，因为她今年十二岁。

### 测验 2（对应录音第 6 句）｜ 填空

**题目：** Complete the sentence: We need some ______. They can make my room beautiful.

**答案：** balloons

**解析：** 对话第 6 轮 Anna 说需要一些气球来装扮房间，气球用 balloon，some 后填复数 balloons。

### 测验 3（对应录音第 8 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** What drink do they buy?

- A. Some orange juice
- B. Some milk
- C. Some water
- D. Some cola

**答案：** A. Some orange juice

**解析：** 对话第 8 轮 Anna 说 'let's get some orange juice, too.'，他们还要买一些橙汁。

### 测验 4（对应录音第 10 句）｜ 单选

**题目：** How much is the cake?

- A. Fifteen yuan
- B. Fifty yuan
- C. Forty yuan
- D. Five yuan

**答案：** B. Fifty yuan

**解析：** 对话第 10 轮 Anna 说 'It's fifty yuan.'，蛋糕五十元，不贵。
