## 期中冲刺 · Starter–Unit 3

- **副标题：** 覆盖 Starter Units 1–3 与 Unit 1–3 的词汇、语法、听力与阅读
- **覆盖单元：** Starter Unit 1、Starter Unit 2、Starter Unit 3、Unit 1、Unit 2、Unit 3
- **建议时长：** 20 分钟 ｜ **总分：** 100 分 ｜ **题数：** 14

#### 第 1 题（q-e1-01）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Starter Unit 2

**题目：** 你找不到自己的橡皮了，可以向同学问：

**选项：**

- A. Where is my eraser?
- B. What's this in English?
- C. How are you?
- D. Nice to meet you!

**答案：** A. Where is my eraser?

**解析：** 询问物品在哪里用 Where is...?；eraser 意为"橡皮"，是 Starter Unit 2 的重点文具词。

#### 第 2 题（q-e1-02）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 1

**题目：** 选出下列单词的中文意思：China。

**选项：**

- A. 中国
- B. 英国
- C. 美国
- D. 加拿大

**答案：** A. 中国

**解析：** China 意为"中国"；英国是 the UK，美国是 the US，注意不要混淆。

#### 第 3 题（q-e1-03）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 2

**题目：** My mother's mother is my ___.

**选项：**

- A. grandmother
- B. grandfather
- C. aunt
- D. sister

**答案：** A. grandmother

**解析：** 妈妈的妈妈是外婆（奶奶），英文是 grandmother；grandfather 指爷爷（外公）。

#### 第 4 题（q-e1-04）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 3

**题目：** We have lunch in the school ___.

**选项：**

- A. dining hall
- B. library
- C. gym
- D. art room

**答案：** A. dining hall

**解析：** 吃午饭的地方是"餐厅"dining hall；library 是图书馆，gym 是体育馆，art room 是美术教室。

#### 第 5 题（q-e1-05）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Starter Unit 1

**题目：** Hello! I ___ Peter. What's your name?

**选项：**

- A. am
- B. is
- C. are
- D. be

**答案：** A. am

**解析：** 主语是 I 时 be 动词用 am，介绍自己说 I am Peter.，可缩写为 I'm Peter.。

#### 第 6 题（q-e1-06）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 1

**题目：** —Is the girl your sister? —Yes, ___ is.

**选项：**

- A. she
- B. he
- C. it
- D. they

**答案：** A. she

**解析：** the girl 指女性，回答时用主格代词 she；he 指男性，it 指物，they 指复数。

#### 第 7 题（q-e1-07）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 2

**题目：** This is ___ schoolbag.

**选项：**

- A. Anna's
- B. Anna
- C. Annas
- D. Anna is

**答案：** A. Anna's

**解析：** "Anna 的书包"要在人名后加 's 构成名词所有格；撇号不能省略，Annas 和 Anna is 都是错误形式。

#### 第 8 题（q-e1-08）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 3

**题目：** ___ two blackboards in our classroom.

**选项：**

- A. There are
- B. There is
- C. It is
- D. They are

**答案：** A. There are

**解析：** there be 句型表示"某处有某物"，two blackboards 是复数，be 动词要用 are，即 There are...。

#### 第 9 题（q-e1-09）｜ 听力 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 3

**题目：** 听录音，选择正确答案。Where is the music room?

**录音原文：**

> W: Where is the music room?
> M: It's next to the art room.

**选项：**

- A. It's next to the art room.
- B. It's next to the library.
- C. It's behind the art room.

**答案：** A. It's next to the art room.

**解析：** 对话中 It's next to the art room. 说明音乐室紧挨着美术教室；next to 意为"紧挨着"。

#### 第 10 题（q-e1-10）｜ 听力 ｜ 单选 ｜ Starter Unit 1

**题目：** 听录音，选择正确答案。How is Peter?

**录音原文：**

> W: Good morning, Ms Gao!
> M: Good morning, Peter! How are you?
> W: I'm fine, thanks.

**选项：**

- A. He is fine.
- B. He is five.
- C. He is good at English.

**答案：** A. He is fine.

**解析：** Peter 回答 I'm fine, thanks，说明他很好；fine 意为"健康的；好的"，是问候 How are you? 的常用回答。

#### 第 11 题（q-e1-11）｜ 听力 ｜ 单选 ｜ Starter Unit 2

**题目：** 听录音，选择正确答案。Whose pen is it?

**录音原文：**

> W: Excuse me. Is this your pen?
> M: No, it isn't. It's Helen's.

**选项：**

- A. It's Helen's.
- B. It's Peter's.
- C. It's the girl's.

**答案：** A. It's Helen's.

**解析：** 对话最后一句 It's Helen's. 说明钢笔是海伦的；Helen's 是名词所有格，意为"海伦的"。

#### 第 12 题（q-e1-12）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 1

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Hello, everyone!
> My name is Li Hua. I am twelve years old.
> I am from Beijing, China. I am in Class 3, Grade 7.
> My English teacher is Ms White. She is from Australia.
> She is very nice, and I like her classes very much.
> 
> What class is Li Hua in?

**选项：**

- A. Class 3, Grade 7.
- B. Class 7, Grade 3.
- C. Class 3, Grade 8.
- D. Class 5, Grade 7.

**答案：** A. Class 3, Grade 7.

**解析：** 由 I am in Class 3, Grade 7. 可知李华在七年级 3 班；英语中先说班级再说年级，与中文顺序相反。

#### 第 13 题（q-e1-13）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 2

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Look at this photo of my family.
> The man in blue is my father. He is a doctor.
> The woman next to him is my mother. She is a nurse.
> They work in the same hospital.
> The little boy is my brother Eric. He is only four years old.
> I love my family.
> 
> What does the writer's father do?

**选项：**

- A. He is a doctor.
- B. He is a nurse.
- C. He is a teacher.
- D. He is a farmer.

**答案：** A. He is a doctor.

**解析：** 由 The man in blue is my father. He is a doctor. 可知爸爸是医生；nurse（护士）是妈妈的工作，不要混淆。

#### 第 14 题（q-e1-14）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 3

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Welcome to my school!
> There are five buildings in it.
> The classroom building is in the middle of the school.
> The library is behind it, and the dining hall is next to it.
> There is a big sports field too.
> We often play football there after class.
> I love my school.
> 
> Where is the dining hall?

**选项：**

- A. Next to the library.
- B. Behind the library.
- C. In the middle of the school.
- D. Next to the sports field.

**答案：** A. Next to the library.

**解析：** 文中 the dining hall is next to it，其中 it 指 the library，说明餐厅紧挨着图书馆；in the middle of the school 的是教学楼。

## 期末冲刺 · Unit 4–Unit 7

#### 第 1 题（q-e2-01）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 4

#### 第 2 题（q-e2-02）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 5

**题目：** Li Ming likes singing. He can join the ___ club.

**答案：** A. music

#### 第 4 题（q-e2-04）｜ 词汇 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 7

- B. 十一月

#### 第 5 题（q-e2-05）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 4

**题目：** I like maths ___ it is fun and useful.

**选项：**

- A. because
- B. but
- C. so
- D. or

**答案：** A. because

**解析：** it is fun and useful 说明的是喜欢数学的原因，要用 because 引导原因；but 表转折，so 表结果。

#### 第 6 题（q-e2-06）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 5

**题目：** Peter can play chess, but he ___ swim.

**选项：**

- A. can't
- B. can
- C. does
- D. isn't

**答案：** A. can't

**解析：** but 表转折：会下棋但不会游泳；can 的否定形式是 can't（cannot），后接动词原形。

#### 第 7 题（q-e2-07）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 6

**题目：** —___ do you usually go to school? —At seven thirty.

**选项：**

- A. What time
- B. What
- C. Where
- D. Who

**答案：** A. What time

**解析：** 回答 At seven thirty 是具体时间点，询问"几点"用 What time；What 问事物，Where 问地点，Who 问人。

#### 第 8 题（q-e2-08）｜ 语法 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 7

**题目：** May is the ___ month of the year.

**选项：**

- A. fifth
- B. five
- C. fifteen
- D. fifty

**答案：** A. fifth

**解析：** "第五个月"要用序数词 fifth；five 是基数词"五"，变序数词时 five 要把 ve 变 f 再加 th，即 fifth。

#### 第 11 题（q-e2-11）｜ 听力 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 6

**题目：** 听录音，选择正确答案。What time does Tom usually get up?

**录音原文：**

> W: What time do you usually get up, Tom?
> M: I usually get up at six forty.

**选项：**

- A. At six forty.
- B. At six thirty.
- C. At seven forty.

**答案：** A. At six forty.

**解析：** 由 I usually get up at six forty 可知 Tom 通常六点四十起床；six thirty（六点半）和 seven forty（七点四十）是易混的干扰时间。

#### 第 12 题（q-e2-12）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 4

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Hi, I'm Gina.
> Today is Monday, and I have maths and science.
> They are my favourite subjects.
> Our maths teacher Mr Chen makes his classes fun.
> Science is difficult, but I like it because I can do experiments.
> My deskmate Alan likes PE best. He plays basketball every day.
> 
> Why does Gina like science?

**选项：**

- A. Because she can do experiments.
- B. Because it is easy.
- C. Because her maths teacher is fun.
- D. Because Alan likes it.

**答案：** A. Because she can do experiments.

**解析：** 由 I like it because I can do experiments 可知，Gina 喜欢科学是因为能做实验；文中说科学 difficult，并不简单。

#### 第 13 题（q-e2-13）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 6

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Amy is a student in Grade 7.
> She gets up at six thirty and has breakfast at seven.
> Classes begin at eight and finish at four in the afternoon.
> After school, she goes to the music club and plays the violin.
> In the evening, she does her homework first.
> She goes to bed at nine.
> 
> What does Amy do after school?

**选项：**

- A. She plays the violin in the music club.
- B. She does her homework first.
- C. She goes to bed at nine.
- D. She has breakfast at seven.

**答案：** A. She plays the violin in the music club.

**解析：** 由 After school, she goes to the music club and plays the violin 可知，Amy 放学后去音乐社团拉小提琴；做作业和睡觉是晚上的安排。

#### 第 14 题（q-e2-14）｜ 阅读 ｜ 单选 ｜ Unit 7

> 读下面的短文，选择正确答案。
> Hi, I'm Bill.
> My birthday is on the tenth of June. It is my favourite day of the year.
> Every year, my mother makes a big birthday cake for me.
> My father gives me some books as presents.
> My best friend Kate comes to my party too.
> We sing the birthday song and play games together.
> 
> When is Bill's birthday?

**选项：**

- A. On the tenth of June.
- B. On the tenth of July.
- C. On the first of June.
- D. On the twentieth of June.

**答案：** A. On the tenth of June.

**解析：** 由 My birthday is on the tenth of June 可知，Bill 的生日是六月十日；七月十日、六月一日等均为干扰项。
